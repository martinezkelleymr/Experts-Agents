How it works

One shared process, not 15 separate chatbots.

  1. 1

    The Founder asks

    "Should we flip this house or rent it?" or "What should we build on this lot?"

  2. 2

    The CEO Orchestrator plans

    It chooses the right team of experts and the order they work in.

  3. 3

    The experts collaborate

    Each writes its report in a shared Deal File and reads the others' reports to build on their work.

  4. 4

    The Founder decides

    The Orchestrator delivers one Decision Memo. Nothing moves forward without the Founder's approval.

      Built-in safety rules
      
        
      The agents recommend. People decide. Licensed professionals do licensed work.
      
        
      
          
      Only the Founder approves
      No agent can approve, sign, pay or send anything. Approval is enforced in code.
      
          
      Compliance can stop a deal
      The Risk & Compliance Officer blocks deals that break company rules. Try the condo demo.
      
          
      Drafts, never sent
      After approval, offers and letters of intent are drafts for an attorney or broker to review.
      
          
      Numbers from tested formulas
      All math runs through tested calculators, and the CFO agent double-checks the profit.
      
          
      Hidden instructions are ignored
      Text such as "ignore your instructions" inside a document is flagged as a security issue, not followed.
      
          
      Licensed work stays human
      Appraisals, inspections, engineering, construction and legal work are listed for Florida-licensed professionals.
      
        
      
      
      
    
      

    
    
      
      
      
        
      
          
      About this project
      
          
      MK.Buildings is a planned South Florida residential investment and development company. The research behind it covers Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach market data, Florida regulations, financing and the professional roles a real estate firm needs. That research shaped these 15 AI experts.
      
          
      The agents are written in Python. To keep this site free on Netlify, the Python agents are run ahead of time on sample deals and their real outputs are saved for this page. No paid AI service or API is used.
